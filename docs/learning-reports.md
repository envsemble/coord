# Learning guide

The diagram remains the default. **Understand the model**, in both the Atlas app and offline HTML, opens the model's guide beside it: its walkthroughs, each step linking to its view and elements, and its finite examples with their assessment under the declared scope; OntoUML models add their checks, open questions and stereotype lessons. An element's details hold its rationale, open questions, linked planning passages and source evidence. Closing the guide keeps the camera and selection. Source dates and coverage stay on the provenance label; the offline viewer's SVG downloads provide figures.

An optional `learning` field contains `walkthroughs` and `scenarios`. Walkthroughs have identified titles and steps with `text`, `elementIds` and an optional explicit `viewKey`. `contextIds` references retained planning records. Scenarios have `id`, `title`, `expected: "valid" | "invalid"`, `instances: [{id, classIds}]` and `links: [{relationId, sourceId, targetId}]`, plus optional description/context. Optional `reporting: {scope, author?}` specifies submission scope. All references are checked before storage; `atlas_revise` updates these fields with the expected revision. See [the authored enrollment example](../packages/diagrams/examples/enrollment/learning.cjs).

`checkScenario(document, id)` and paginated `atlas_scenarios` assess finite examples using inherited memberships, both relation-end numeric counts and complete/disjoint generalization sets. A count beside the target is counted for each source, and vice versa. Duplicate link pairs are rejected. An unknown count, type-use dependency or textual invariant stays unassessed. Known violations can establish a counterexample even when other constraints are unassessed. Expectation mismatch is reported; it never silently changes the authored example.

These are closed-world examples over the explicitly listed population, not a general solver or a proof of domain validity. They do not establish temporal identity, rigidity, arbitrary OCL/SQL, framework behavior or implementation equivalence. OntoUML learning guidance is based on the [community specification](https://ontouml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), including its [phase-partition constraints](https://ontouml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/classes/sortals/phase/index.html). Existing local structural/ontology rules retain their documented subset coverage.

The guide and its examples are embedded in the same versioned HTML document. Reopening retains learning, scope, selected context and frozen captures. Imported dates/hashes remain preserved claims until source verification. No external assets or model service are needed to read the guide; specification/source links are optional navigation. Keeping a plan passage in a model does not transfer ownership from its planning record.
